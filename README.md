# LSDV-Mymensingh-Bangladesh
Molecular Detection and Characterization of Lumpy Skin Disease Virus in cattle from Mymensingh District, Bangladesh. 

## Overview
Skin nodule samples were collected from clinically suspected cattle in
Mymensingh District. LSDV was detected by PCR targeting the P32 gene
(192 bp), the virus was isolated on the chorioallantoic membrane (CAM) of
embryonated eggs, and selected samples were sequenced and analyzed
phylogenetically.

## Methods
1. Sample collection: skin nodules from clinically suspected cattle
2. PCR detection of the P32 gene
3. Virus isolation: CAM inoculation of embryonated eggs
4. Sequencing and phylogenetic analysis

## Software
- CLC Genomics Workbench: sequence editing
- MAFFT: multiple sequence alignment
- MEGA-12: phylogenetic tree construction
- iTOL: tree visualization
